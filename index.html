About Me –

Hello! I'm Biku, a passionate student diving deep into the world of coding and modern technologies. Like many others, my journey didn't begin in a classroom — it started with simple curiosity: How do websites work? What makes an app run? How can a few lines of text become something functional and useful? Those questions led me here. Right now, I'm focused on building a strong foundation in programming. I'm learning how to think like a developer — logically, patiently, and creatively. Whether it's writing my first lines of Python, understanding how JavaScript brings web pages to life, or exploring the logic behind databases and APIs, I find joy in every small breakthrough. Debugging, though frustrating at times, has become one of my favorite teachers. Every error message is just a puzzle waiting to be solved. But coding isn't just about syntax or frameworks for me. It's about problem-solving. It's about taking an idea — no matter how messy — and turning it into something real. I love the process of breaking big problems into smaller pieces, and the satisfaction of seeing something finally work after hours of effort never gets old. I'm also fascinated by emerging technologies. From cloud computing to artificial intelligence, I'm constantly reading, watching tutorials, and working on small side projects to keep learning. I believe technology should be accessible, useful, and well-crafted. That's the kind of developer I want to become. This space — whether it's my portfolio, GitHub, or blog — is where I share my progress, my projects, and my thoughts. I'm not an expert yet, but I'm on my way. And I'm always open to feedback, collaboration, or just a good tech conversation. Thanks for stopping by, Biku
